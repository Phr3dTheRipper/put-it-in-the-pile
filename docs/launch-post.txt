Title: I built a Codex usage meter because apparently we were running low

After seeing only several thousand Codex usage meters, I noticed a gap in the
market: none of them were the one I had personally made.

Introducing PUT IT IN THE PILE.

It displays your Codex usage in a small window above your other windows.
I know. Take a moment.

Features:
- The same numbers you already had access to, now in my rectangle.
- Always on top, reflecting the importance of this contribution.
- A rigorously maintained 0% originality score.
- Compact mode. The same lack of innovation, in less space.
- A button labeled "PUT IT IN THE PILE" that adds another usage meter to a pile
  of usage meters. Finally, a feature designed around the community's feedback.

It actually reads your real Codex quota. I was going to make the numbers up,
but then it would have had a differentiator.

No waitlist. No monetization strategy. No reason.

Put it in the pile.
